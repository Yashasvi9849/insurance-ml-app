This letter is an official 30 day notice to amend your rental agreement, as of September 01, 2024. Effective September 01,
2024 a minimum of one tenant per Rental Agreement is required to carry and maintain $100,000 of property damage liability
insurance. The resident is responsible for damages caused by the resident or the resident’s guests or pets or animals. We are
excited to let you know that we have partnered with Your Renters Insurance Group (YRIG) to manage all your renter's insurance
requirements. This helps protect you in the event of a covered loss or catastrophe. To satisfy this requirement, please choose
from one of the options outlined below.
1. The property manager's Master Policy Program (MPP) program through the payment of $16.00 in accordance with the
terms of your residential rental agreement.
• MPP is designed to fulfill the insurance requirement of the Rental Agreement. Landlord is the Insured under the MPP. This is
single interest force placed insurance. Resident is not an Insured, Additional Insured or beneficiary under the MPP. All loss
payments are made to the Landlord. MPP coverage is NOT personal liability insurance or renter’s insurance.
• In the event that loss or damage to Landlord’s property exceeds the amount of Required Insurance, Resident shall remain
contractually liable to Landlord for such amount. In the event of liability to any other party for bodily injury or property damage,
Resident shall remain liable to such other party.

charged an additional rent of $16.00 per month. If the required coverage should be canceled or lapse for any reason, Johnson
Property Mgmt, LLC reserves the right to place your unit in the MPP and charge you an additional rent of $16.00 per month.

update your policy, or login to your insurance portal and update the policy yourself. You may choose to work with your
