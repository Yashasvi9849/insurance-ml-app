WHEREAS Tenant has expressed a desire to pay a recurring, non-refundable fee as stated
below in lieu of payment of a security deposit; NOW THEREFORE, the Parties mutually
covenant and agree as follows:
1. PAYMENT OF FEE: The Parties agree Tenant will pay Landlord the Fee Amount,
stated below, on a recurring basis at the same time each rent payment is due during the term
of the Lease in lieu of the payment of a security deposit. The Fee Amount in lieu of a security
deposit is being paid only to secure occupancy of the Premises without a requirement to pay
a security deposit. Specifically, the payment of the Fee Amount, as set forth in this Section,
will supersede the obligation to pay the security deposit in Section 10(a) of the Lease. The fee
in lieu of a security deposit is not paying for insurance that covers Tenant, or otherwise
changes Tenant's obligation to pay Rent and damages, as set forth in the Lease, beyond
normal wear and tear.
2. NON-REFUNDABLE: All Fee Amounts paid are not refundable, and in the event of
termination of the Lease for any reason, payment of the Fee Amount in lieu of a security
deposit does not eliminate, release, or otherwise limit Tenant’s responsibilities under the
Lease.
3. RIGHT TO PAY SECURITY DEPOSIT. During any point of the Term of the Lease,
Tenant may withdraw this agreement to pay the Fee Amount in lieu of a security deposit, and
instead Tenant will be charged the security deposit in the amount set forth Section 10(a) of
the Lease, which will be due and payable on the date the Tenant chooses to withdraw this
agreement to pay the Fee Amount.
