Johnson Property Management, LLC is pleased to offer residents with this program as an alternative to
paying traditional security deposits for rental properties. Benefit to residents include:
1) Reduced move-in costs in exchange for a small monthly fee.
2) Resident's money is available to be used elsewhere.
3) Only pay what is owed at lease-end instead of having money locked up for entire lease term.
( X ) I/We agree to pay $45.00 monthly in lieu of making an up-front security deposit.
( ) I/We would like to make the traditional security deposit required by Owner.
In lieu of the security deposit, Owner offers to waive the one-time security deposit in exchange for the payment of the
monthly service charge indicated above for the full duration of the Rental Agreement. In the event the Property
Management contract between Owner and Agent is terminated, the monthly service charge referenced herein will cease
and the Property Owner may require that you pay a traditional security deposit. If Tenant(s) choose to pay the Zero
Deposit Program monthly cost in lieu of the advertised property deposit, Tenant(s) agree and understand the following:
1. The monthly amount paid does NOT accumulate or IS NOT in any way considered or related to a security deposit.
2. Tenant(s) understand that the monthly amount paid is a convenience service that allows them to move their costs
from the front of the tenancy to the end of the tenancy, and will need to pay non-normal wear move out costs upon
vacancy.
3. Tenant(s) understands that carpets will be professionally cleaned upon Rental Agreement Termination and charged
to Tenant's rental account.
4. Tenant(s) understands that other than carpet cleaning, property MUST be left in move-in ready rental condition as
decided by Agent. Move-in condition means equal to the condition of the property at time of Key Transfer, normal
wear and tear accepted.
5. Tenant(s) understand that if property is not left in move-in ready condition, all non-normal wear & tear costs
associated with making it equal to move-in ready condition will be charged to Tenant's rental account, examples of
which include general cleaning, flooring repair or replacement, wall repair and painting in excess of normal wear and
tear, key or lock replacement, non working light bulb replacement, dirty furnace filter replacement, general repair in
excess of normal wear and tear, landscaping cleanup, hauling of abandoned property, in addition to any unpaid rent,
charges, or fees left unpaid on Tenant's Account.
6. Tenant(s) understand that within 30 days of the Termination of Rental Agreement, a Final Account Statement will be AG
produced and emailed and physically mailed to the Tenant's forward address if one is provided.
7. Tenant(s) understand that within 7 days of the sending of the emailed and mailed Final Account Statement,
Tenant MUST pay amount owed on Final Account Statement. If full account balance is not paid, account
will be assigned to a professional debt collection agency.
