Addendum to Residential Lease Agreement
This Addendum is attached to and is specifically made a part of the Residential Lease Agreement
between:
Brian Rice Kirk Wayne McGee, Kelly Anne McGee
(Owner) and (Tenants)
When signed by the Tenant, this Addendum becomes a material part of the Residential Lease Agreement
between the parties named above. The Tenant has read and understands the Residential Lease
Agreement.
The Residential Lease Agreement is amended as follows and Page 12, Paragraph 26. SPECIAL
PROVISIONS to reads:
Specialized Property Management will have the carpet professionally cleaned and property professionally
cleaned by a preferred vendor at fair market rates at move-out, the cost for both will be charged to the
tenant; There is no smoking allowed inside the property, this includes the garage; Specialized has access
to the property; There is a zero tolerance policy on NSF or returned payments, RentMoney will be
required going forward; In the event an eviction is filed, $350.00 fee will be added to the tenant’s account,
regardless of the outcome of the filing, and must be paid in full along with outstanding balance in order to
dismiss the filing; $35.00 fee will be added to the tenant’s account for any notice that is sent to the
residence by Specialized, including but not limited to Notice to Vacates or Violation Notices; The lockbox
is to remain at the property during the tenancy and turned into the office at move-out, $75.00 charge if the
lockbox is lost, stolen, or misplaced, applicable only if lockbox was provided at move-in; Tenant is
responsible for replacing all A/C and heating system filters at the property on a monthly basis, Filters are
provided by Specialized; The filters will be mailed directly to the property; Specialized will provide
assistance/training to tenant on the proper replacement of the filters, upon written request from tenant;
Tenant’s failure to properly replace the filters in a timely manner is a material breach of this agreement
and Specialized shall be entitled to exercise its right to remedy the breach by accessing the premises,
following reasonable notice and at
tenant’s expense, to replace the air filters at a cost to the tenant of $125.00; Tenant hereby acknowledges
that the filters are dated to verify replacement and are subject to inspection by Specialized upon
reasonable notice. Tenant shall remain liable to the Landlord for all damages to the A/C or heating
system caused by tenant’s failure to replace the filters correctly; The program fee is $17.50 per month
and is to be paid with rent; If not paid, a portion of the rent payment will be applied to this charge prior to
applying the payment to the rent charge; the filters will be shipped in quarterly shipments to the rental
property.
All other terms of the Residential Lease Agreement remain unchanged and in effect.
